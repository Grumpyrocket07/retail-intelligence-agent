# Product Care and Safety

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Ceramics and glassware
We recommend hand washing all ceramics and glassware. Items are dishwasher safe or microwave safe only when the product page or base marking says so. Avoid sudden changes of temperature with glassware.

## Candles
Burn candles for no more than 3 to 4 hours at a time and trim the wick to about 5 mm before each use. Never leave a burning candle unattended, and keep candles away from children and pets.

## Wooden items
Wipe wooden items with a damp cloth and dry them straight away. Never soak wooden items or put them in a dishwasher.

## Cushions and textiles
Cushion covers are sold without pads. Matching cushion pads can be bought separately from the same product range. Wash covers and throws at 30 degrees unless the care label says otherwise.

## Children's items
Toys and children's items are not suitable for children under 3 years unless the product page says so. All toys carry UKCA or CE safety marking.
