# General Questions

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Do you have a shop?
No. Cottage Lane Gifts is an online-only retailer and has no physical shop.

## Contacting customer support
Our customer support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm UK time, and replies within 1 working day.

## Gift wrapping and messages
Gift wrapping costs £2.50 per item. A printed gift message of up to 200 characters is free.

## Gift vouchers
Gift vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase and cannot be refunded.

## Changing an order
You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it. After that, contact customer support as soon as possible, and we will try to stop the order before it is dispatched.

## Out of stock items
Out of stock items are restocked every 2 to 3 weeks. You can sign up for an email alert on the product page. We do not price match other retailers.
