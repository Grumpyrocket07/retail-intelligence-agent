# Damaged or Missing Items

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Reporting damage
If an item arrives damaged, tell us within 48 hours of delivery and send photos of both the item and the packaging. We will send a replacement or give a refund. For damaged items worth less than £10 we do not ask for the item to be sent back.

## Missing items
If something is missing from your parcel, tell us within 7 days of delivery. We check the packing record and send missing items at no charge.

## Parcel marked as delivered but not received
If tracking shows a parcel as delivered but you have not received it, contact us within 5 working days. We investigate with the courier, which can take up to 10 working days. We send a replacement or refund once the investigation is complete.

## Damage in wholesale cartons
Breakages in wholesale cartons must also be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the carton and the broken items.
