# Payment and Invoicing

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Wholesale customers can also pay by bank transfer. We accept payment in pounds sterling only.

## When you are charged
Your card is charged when you place your order. If a payment fails, we hold the order for 24 hours so that you can try another payment method, and then cancel it.

## VAT
Retail prices on the website include VAT at 20%. A VAT invoice is emailed when your order is dispatched.

## Invoice and credit note numbers
Invoice numbers have six digits. Credit notes, which record returns and cancellations, use the invoice number with the letter C in front of it.

## Guest orders
Customers can check out as a guest without creating an account. Guest orders are not linked to a customer account, so they do not appear in your order history.
