# Customer Account and Privacy

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Your account
You can create an account with your email address to see your order history and print return labels. A password reset link is valid for 1 hour.

## How long we keep your data
We keep order records for 6 years because tax law requires it. If you ask us to delete your account, we process the request within 30 days, but order records are kept for the 6 years required.

## Marketing emails
Marketing emails are opt-in only. Every email contains an unsubscribe link. We never sell personal data to other companies.

## Data requests
You can ask for a copy of your personal data through the privacy form on our website. We reply within one month.
