# International Orders and Customs

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Where we deliver
We deliver to over 40 countries, including Ireland, Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Switzerland, Norway, Australia, the USA, Japan and South Africa.

## International delivery times and prices
Delivery to EU countries takes 5 to 8 working days and starts from £12.95. Delivery to the rest of the world takes 7 to 14 working days and starts from £19.95. EU orders of £200 or more are delivered free.

## Customs, duties and import VAT
For deliveries outside the UK, the customer is responsible for any import duties, taxes and customs fees charged by the destination country. We cannot mark goods as gifts or declare a lower value.

## International returns
The 30-day return window applies to international orders too, but the customer pays the return postage and any customs paperwork.
