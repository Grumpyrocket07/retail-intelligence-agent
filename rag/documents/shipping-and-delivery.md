# Shipping and Delivery

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Dispatch times
Orders placed before 2 pm UK time, Monday to Friday, are dispatched the same working day. Orders placed after 2 pm, or at weekends, are dispatched the next working day. We do not dispatch on Saturdays, Sundays or UK bank holidays. A tracking link is emailed as soon as your parcel is dispatched.

## UK delivery options
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs £4.95. Standard delivery is free on retail orders of £50 or more. Express delivery costs £9.95 and delivers the next working day for orders placed before 2 pm.

## Scottish Highlands, Islands and Northern Ireland
Deliveries to the Scottish Highlands, the islands and Northern Ireland take 1 to 2 working days longer than the times shown above and carry a surcharge of £3.00 per order.

## Delivery attempts
Our couriers make up to 2 delivery attempts. After the second failed attempt the parcel is held at the local depot for 7 days and then returned to us. If a parcel is returned to us we will contact you to arrange redelivery, which is charged at the standard delivery rate.
