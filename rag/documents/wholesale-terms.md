# Wholesale Terms

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Opening a wholesale account
Wholesale accounts are available to registered businesses. The minimum first order is £250. Every later order has a minimum of £150. Wholesale prices are shown excluding VAT, and VAT is added at checkout.

## Wholesale discounts
Wholesale customers receive a discount on our retail prices. Orders from £250 to £999 receive 35% off retail prices. Orders of £1,000 or more receive 40% off retail prices. Many products are sold in cartons of 6, 12 or 24 units, and the carton size is shown on each product page.

## Payment terms
New wholesale accounts pay in full when they order. After 3 successful orders an account can move to invoice terms of 30 days, with an initial credit limit of £2,000. Accounts that are more than 14 days overdue are put on hold until the balance is paid.

## Wholesale delivery
Wholesale orders of £500 or more are delivered free within the UK. Smaller wholesale orders are charged at the standard delivery rate.

## Wholesale returns
Wholesale returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery, and only for faulty goods or goods sent in error. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted on wholesale orders.
