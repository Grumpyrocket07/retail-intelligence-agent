# Seasonal Ordering and Christmas Deadlines

> Fictional demo document for the Retail Intelligence Platform project. Cottage Lane Gifts is not a real company.

## Busy season
Our busy season runs from September to December, and November is our busiest month. During this period dispatch can take 1 to 2 working days longer than usual.

## Christmas last order dates for retail customers
To arrive before Christmas, order by 15 December for UK standard delivery, or by 21 December before 2 pm for UK express delivery. The last order dates are 8 December for the EU and 1 December for the rest of the world.

## Christmas last order dates for wholesale customers
We recommend that wholesale customers place Christmas orders by 31 October. The final date for guaranteed delivery before Christmas is 20 November.

## Extended Christmas returns
Retail items bought between 1 November and 24 December can be returned until 31 January, instead of the usual 30 days. This extended window does not apply to wholesale orders.
